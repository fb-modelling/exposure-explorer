University funding reform: exposure explorer

2024/25 HESA finance data. The explorer shows the 239 providers assessed on a comparable basis; every figure on the page is computed from them. Money figures are valued at the year-three peak of the phased shock profile (entrants fall immediately; fee income responds over course lengths, and some VAT-deterred demand returns), matching the accompanying paper. Select a policy to see how its effect is distributed; click a region to filter, and click any university for its full position, the calculation behind it, and the fee it would need to charge. Built to inform how a transition would be designed, not to predict outcomes.

Regional impact

For each region, the top bar is the share of providers in deficit, the lower bar the share whose deficit is deeper than 10% of income. English regions are shown on providers with usable tariff data only, matching the paper: providers without it carry the VAT and grant components alone, so including them would mix two different shocks and flatter the least-covered regions. Grey is the position before the reforms; colour is what the selected policy adds. Click a region to filter the list.
